Workflows

Four delivery paths.

Pick the amount of structure you need without changing the operating model.

01 · Full control

High rigor

  • 🤖 LLM: product spec, tech spec, build plan, implementation, test cases, E2E, manual QA, retro.
  • 👤 Human: reviews every artifact and decides when the next stage starts.
  • Best for: important, ambiguous, or architectural work.

02 · Semi-control

Faster feature work

  • 🤖 LLM: create-tech-spec, then implement-feature with task plans and QA gates.
  • 👤 Human: approves the direction and reviews the final QA evidence.
  • Best for: clear feature work that still needs discipline.

03 · Quick feature

Tiny scoped change

  • 🤖 LLM: investigates, gates the scope, writes one plan, implements, and verifies.
  • 👤 Human: approves the single plan before coding starts.
  • Best for: local work that should stay small.

04 · Bugfix

Focused fix path

  • 🤖 LLM: turns the issue into a focused fix plan, implements, and verifies.
  • 👤 Human: approves the plan and reviews the evidence.
  • Best for: defects that need a disciplined repair path.

  - "*.env"
  - "**/*.env"

  - code
  - "{worktreeDir}"
